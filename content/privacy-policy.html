Privacy Policy

NextRow takes our customer privacy seriously. The following is offered to help our customers understand our privacy policy. What Our privacy policy Covers

This policy covers how NextRow treats personal information that NextRow collects and receives, including information related to past use of NextRow services. Personal information is information about our customers that is personally identifiable like names, addresses, email addresses, or phone numbers, and that is not otherwise publicly available.

This policy does not apply to the practices of companies that NextRow does not own or control, or to people that NextRow does not employ or manage.

Information Collection and Use

NextRow collects personal information when you become a customer for web design, SEO or hosting services. NextRow may combine information about our customers that we have with information we may obtain from business partners or other companies.

We ask customers for information such as name, email address, ZIp code, occupation, industry, and personal account preferences.

NextRow collects information about customer business transactions with us and with some of our business partners, including information about bank accounts and merchant services accounts.

NextRow uses information on customers to provide better customer service and to fulfill requests and requirements for website design and development projects.

Children

NextRow does not contract with or provide services to children under age 13 without parental knowledge and consent.

Information Sharing and Disclosure

NextRow does not rent, sell, or share personal information about our customers with other people or non-affiliated companies except to provide products or services requested, when we have permission, or under the following circumstances:

We do not share information of customers who are children under age 13 without parental consent.

We respond to subpoenas, court orders, or legal process, or to establish or exercise our legal rights or defend against legal claims.

We believe it is necessary to share information in order to investigate, prevent, or take action regarding illegal activities, suspected fraud, situations involving potential threats to the physical safety of any person, violations of NextRow’s terms of use, or as otherwise required by law.

We transfer information about our customers if NextRow is acquired by or merged with another company. In this event, NextRow will notify customers before information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

NextRow works with vendors, partners, advertisers, and other service providers in different industries and categories of business. We provide information on these partners regarding providers of products or services that any customer may request.

Cookies

NextRow designed and developed websites may set and access cookies on user’s computers.

Customer Ability to Edit and Delete Account Information and Preferences

Customers can generally edit their website Content Management System Account Information, and contact information at any time.

NextRow reserves the right to send customers communications relating to NextRow services, such as service announcements, administrative messages that are considered part of a customer’s service account, without offering the opportunity to opt out of receiving them.

NextRow customers may not delete their website design, development or hosting accounts or projects without first contacting NextRow through any of our published contact methods.

Children

NextRow does not grant children access to manage accounts without parental permission and controls.

Parents may choose to add, edit or delete their children’s account using their master administrative access under their domain or web hosting account services

Confidentiality and Security

NextRow limits access to personal information about customers to our employees who we believe reasonably need to come into contact with that information to provide products or services that have been contracted for.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

NextRow may update this policy. We will notify customers about significant changes in the way we treat personal information by sending a notice to the primary email address specified in our customer account or by placing a prominent notice on our site.

Questions and Suggestions

Anyone may contact NextRow with privacy policy questions or suggestions, by using our contact form.
